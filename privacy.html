Data Collection

What data we collect

When you ask an instrument for the report

  • Email address
  • Which instrument you used and the language of the page
  • When you first asked, when you last asked, and how many times

The scan result is not part of that record. The report file is built in your browser from what is already on the screen, so the URL you tested, its score and its findings are never sent to us or stored.

When you book via Calendly

  • Name, email, and any notes you enter on Calendly's form
  • Meeting time and timezone

Automatically (every visitor)

  • IP address, user-agent, and request URL (Cloudflare logs — needed to serve pages and prevent abuse)
  • Anonymized analytics: pageviews, device class, referrer — only if you accept the cookie banner

What we do NOT collect

  • Payment card numbers or banking information
  • Government ID numbers
  • Health or biometric data
  • Any "special category" data under GDPR Article 9 / Ley 81 Article 7

Rights

Your rights as a data subject

Whether you're in Panama (Ley 81) or the EU/EEA (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access — a copy of the data we hold about you (GDPR Art. 15 / Ley 81 Art. 13)
  • Rectification — correct anything that's wrong (GDPR Art. 16 / Ley 81 Art. 14)
  • Erasure — delete what we have about you (GDPR Art. 17 / Ley 81 Art. 15)
  • Restriction — pause our processing while a dispute is resolved (GDPR Art. 18)
  • Portability — get your data in a structured machine-readable format (GDPR Art. 20)
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent — reopen the cookie banner via the "Cookies" link in the footer, or email us

To exercise any right, email julio@jldatrum.com with your name, the email you used with us, and a brief description of what you're asking for. We respond within 15 working days (Ley 81) or 30 days (GDPR) — whichever is shorter for your case.

Retention

How long we keep your data

  • Report requests: 24 months, or until you request deletion
  • Calendly booking data: per Calendly's own retention policy (we don't store a copy)
  • Cloudflare request logs: 30 days (rolling), then purged
  • Google Analytics events: 14 months (GA4 default), if you consented
  • Email correspondence with us: indefinitely, unless you ask us to delete it
Consent

What you consent to

By asking an instrument for the report, you explicitly consent to:

  • Storage of your email address in our Cloudflare Worker
  • Generation of the report file in your browser; no scan data is sent with your address
  • Processing by Julio Luque Valdés (DATRUM) for the stated purposes only

Your consent is free (you can use the rest of the site without it), informed (this policy explains it), specific (only for the purposes listed), and revocable (you can withdraw any time).

Cookies

  • Third-party advertising: none. We don't run an ad network or share data with one.

To change your choice, click "Cookies" in the footer to reopen the banner.
