Who's it for?

B2C marketing persuades one person in a moment. B2B marketing arms someone who already wants to hire you with evidence they can defend to a partner, a board, or a client of their own — which makes reach the wrong goal and a verifiable case the right one. I work with businesses whose reputation is already real but whose digital presence carries none of it: high-trust businesses where a buyer vets you online before they ever make contact.

You're a fit if
  • Your reputation is real — but your website, funnel, or content isn't converting it into clients or deals.
  • You want a digital presence that actually matches the caliber of your work.
  • You want qualified clients finding you, not the other way around.
  • You see technology as a distribution tool, not just an administrative one.
Not your fit if
  • You need a one-time brochure with no marketing system behind it.
  • You have no established audience or reputation to build from yet.
  • You're not willing to bring your creative direction — I build the system, but the brand has to be yours.
  • You want to repeat the same generic tactics everyone else runs.
