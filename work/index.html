The DATRUM methodology

The outcome is a presence that earns rankings, references, and trust on its own. The reason it works: I treat security, structure, schema, and strategy as one system — the same disciplines most agencies split across four vendors who never talk.

  1. Secure Infrastructure

    A search engine rewards a hardened site and a serious buyer trusts one. I ship the signals that earn both, on every site. Most agencies never touch this layer.

  2. Heading Architecture

    A per-page heading strategy that maps your industry as a network of information — effortless for search and AI to read. Structure is strategy made visible.

  3. JSON-LD Schema (AI SEO)

    Structured data across your campaigns, content, and products so AI engines quote you directly. The difference between being on the web and being the answer.

  4. Information Architecture

    Creative direction for short- and long-term distribution campaigns, driven by data mining — the editorial eye and the data layer working as one.
