Resources

See what AI can actually read on your site.

When a buyer asks ChatGPT or Gemini who to hire, the answer is assembled from what a machine could read on your website. If it could not read you, you are not ranked lower — you are simply not in the answer. These tools show you what it found.

Free · no account · results not stored

The instruments

Being found takes three things: a page a machine can read, a server that lets it through, and a page a phone can actually load. One instrument measures each. Point all three at your own domain before you point them at anyone else's.

  • AI Visibility Map →

    Paste any URL. It fetches the page, parses it the way an answer engine does, and returns a grade with every deduction itemised. Nothing self-reported. About ten seconds.

  • Responsiveness Radar →

    Paste a URL. It measures what a phone has to download against what a laptop does, and how much of that is pixels no phone can display. Seconds, not adjectives.

  • Trust Index →

    Paste a URL. It grades what your server returns to anyone who asks — the thirteen things a corporate buyer checks before calling back, and the same layer that decides whether a crawler gets served at all. Plain language, no jargon.
