Literary Long-form research · in progress

The instruments above measure. This is the argument behind them: digital legibility, compliance posture, and machine-readable authority now decide who reaches a shortlist — and the businesses filtered out are never told it happened. Failure in this channel is silent, which is precisely what makes it worth writing about.

In the works

  • Compliance is a distribution channel. Why Ley 81 documentation belongs in a growth budget rather than a legal one, and what it opens that marketing spend cannot.
  • The silent disqualifier. A failing security scan removes you from vendor lists without generating a single email. What that costs, and why you never see the invoice.
  • Legibility beats spend. Search rewarded whoever paid most. AI-mediated discovery rewards whoever is most parseable — and that is a one-time build, not a monthly cost.

Until these publish, the instruments are the shortest path to the same conclusions — run them against your own site, or bring findings to a conversation.
